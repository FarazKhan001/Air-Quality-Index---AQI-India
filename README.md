# Air-Quality-Index---AQI-India

## Project Overview
AQI INDIA is a data analysis project focused on understanding air quality conditions across different regions of India. The project studies the Air Quality Index (AQI) and pollutant concentration levels to identify pollution patterns, regional variations, and environmental trends.

By analyzing air quality data at state and city levels, the project highlights highly polluted areas, compares regional air quality, and examines the contribution of major pollutants affecting environmental and public health conditions.

## Objectives
- Analyze air quality trends across India
- Identify the most polluted and least polluted regions
- Study the contribution of major air pollutants
- Understand regional variations in AQI levels
- Promote environmental awareness through data insights

## Dataset Information
The dataset used in this project contains information related to:

- States and Union Territories of India
- Cities and monitoring stations
- Air Quality Index (AQI) values
- Pollutant concentration levels

### Pollutants Analyzed
- PM2.5
- PM10
- Nitrogen Dioxide (NO₂)
- Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂)
- Carbon Monoxide (CO)
- Ozone (O₃)
- Ammonia (NH₃)

## Key Features
- State-wise air quality analysis
- City-level pollution insights
- Identification of pollution hotspots
- AQI category classification
- Comparative environmental analysis across regions

## Project Workflow
1. Data collection and preparation  
2. Data cleaning and standardization  
3. Integration of multiple datasets  
4. Analysis of pollutant concentration levels  
5. Identification of regional pollution patterns  
6. Visualization and interpretation of insights  

## AQI Categories
Air quality levels are classified based on standard AQI categories:

- Good
- Satisfactory
- Moderate
- Poor
- Very Poor

## Project Outcome
The project provides meaningful insights into India's air quality conditions by transforming complex environmental datasets into understandable analytical results. It helps identify pollution trends, regional disparities, and major pollutants impacting air quality.
